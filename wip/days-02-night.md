# A night with the monsters

Status: **open**

The world turns to normal difficulty and the clock runs (or is set to dusk). The robot waits
out the night in its closed, lit hut — a door to go in and out by is part of the hut now — and
fights what gets close (`guard`) when it is out. Zombies hit doors, skeletons shoot, creepers
explode: the robot must notice damage (health from the server), eat when hungry, and be alive
at dawn. Judged: the robot alive, the hut standing, no complaint in the server's log.
