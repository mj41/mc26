# Down to the ores

Status: **open**

A staircase down from the hut: two high, a step down at a time, a torch every few blocks,
water and lava not dug into (a block with a fluid next to it is left, or the fluid closed with
cobblestone first). Stone pickaxe, iron ore (needs it), coal for the furnace, raw iron smelted,
an iron pickaxe; on down to the deepslate (below y 0) and along it until a diamond ore shows,
dug with the iron pickaxe. Judged: the diamond in the inventory, the way back up walkable.
The pathfinder, the dig planner and the physics meet caves, gravel and sand that fall,
deepslate's harder blocks, the dark.
